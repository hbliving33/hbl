The heart of town. Walk the pier, watch the surfers, then head up Main Street for food and shops.

North

Bolsa Chica State Beach

Wide, open sand along Pacific Coast Highway, with fire rings and a long bike path.

Dogs welcome

Huntington Dog Beach

A stretch of shoreline where dogs can run off leash and make friends.

South

Huntington State Beach

Quieter sand running south to the Santa Ana River, with room to spread out.

Beyond the sand

Get outside

Huntington Beach is more than the waves. There are trails, wetlands and a huge city park, all a short ride from the coast.

  • Huntington Central ParkLakes, trails, a library and plenty of shade.
  • Bolsa Chica Ecological ReserveWetland walks and some of the best bird watching on the coast.
  • The beach bike pathMiles of flat riding along the sand, sunrise to sunset.
  • Santa Ana River TrailRide inland from the river mouth along the water.
