    Questions

    Good to know.

    How do I pay?

    After you approve your ad, we send you a secure link to pay by card, monthly or for the year.

    Is there a contract?

    No. Monthly is $10 a month and you can cancel anytime. Yearly is $100 paid up front, which works out to two free months.

    Can I change my ad later?

    Yes. Send us new hours, a new offer or a new photo and we'll update it.

    How does the referral program work?

    Refer a business. When they sign up for a yearly plan and put your business name in the "Referred by" box, you get two free months added to your plan. There's no limit.

    How do I get a free neighborhood site?

    Tell us about your neighborhood, then sign up five local businesses to advertise with HBLiving. Once all five are live, we build your neighborhood site at no cost.

    What are the four areas?

    Downtown and the Pier, Harbour and Sunset Beach, North HB and Central Park, and Southeast HB. They follow Huntington Beach's ZIP codes, so your ad shows in your own area and across the whole city.

    Who sees my ad?

    People looking for local businesses in Huntington Beach and nearby towns: Fountain Valley, Seal Beach, Sunset Beach, Westminster, Costa Mesa and Newport Beach.

    What do I need to get started?

    Just your business name, phone, website or social page, and a sentence or two about what you do. A logo or photo helps for Featured ads.
